# Pack 1 symmetry and source/binary audit

This narrative is authored interpretation of the supplied independent visual review and the reproducible machine evidence in [symmetry_audit.json](symmetry_audit.json). The supplied review reports examining all 30 blueprints and 40 photographs; this generator inventories and hashes those photographs but **does not inspect images** or independently establish visual intent. Hidden geometry is uncertain. The current evidence includes the explicitly authorized bee correction; the audit itself is read-only.

Reproduce from the repository root with installed Node dependencies: run `node schematics/tooling/audit_pack1_symmetry.mjs` and compare stdout with the JSON evidence, or run `node --test test/schematic/pack1_symmetry_audit.test.js`. The JSON includes SHA-256 of all 30 source files, all 40 JPEGs (including the pack-root photograph), all 90 binaries, the manifest, conversion inputs, selected registry data and tooling. It records every whole-volume X and Z midplane and both diagonals for square footprints. For each, it records dimensions, bounds, matching/compared non-air pairs, air-only pairs, coverage, center cells, mismatches by class, out-of-bounds totals, and up to 12 example mismatch coordinates with complete states. The generator does not claim that these are the only possible local planes. The JSON also inventories every door/gate cell and every *adjacent same-material* horizontal door/gate pair; non-adjacent possible architectural pairings require human review.

## Findings and decisions

Nine of 30 have at least one exactly matching whole-volume plane: bee X=4.5; wardrobe X=2.5; enderman and pig balloons X=3.5; silo Z=4; safety fence X=4 and Z=1; turn X=Z; carousel X=8; strider bath X=4. Exact reflection does not establish design correctness. The remaining builds have at least one mismatch or non-mirrorable center state on every tested plane; detailed per-plane counts are in the JSON.

* The bee was explicitly authorized for symmetry correction using the west side as a deterministic assumption, not photographic proof. All 221 pre-correction non-air pairs now match; the current metric is 220/220 because one pair became air-only. The four east cells and reviewed plan are recorded in the [validation note](../../../blueprints/pack1/02_bee_hot_air_balloon.validation.md).
* The barn envelope has a deliberately one-sided pen and offset ventilator. The structural front roof profile, not the complete build, is exactly mirrored on X=6.5 in bounds 0..13, 7..12, 0..0 (7/7 non-air pairs). The full attachment-inclusive region has cells outside that plane's reflected extent. Its bounding-box midplane X=8.5 is separately measured in the JSON. Neither plane justifies changing its asymmetric attachments.
* Allay X=8 has 116/118 matching pairs. The missing information and artistic nature of the statue prevent selecting a side or asserting a defect. No change is proposed.
* The wardrobe's two south-facing open leaves already had mirrored states even when both hinges pointed inward. The prior reviewed reference interpretation calls for outside hinges; the current source has right/left hinges at (2,0,1)/(3,0,1) and the same at y=1. Both current versioned binaries match those four cells. The unsuffixed historic original retains the opposite four hinges and is intentionally preserved; reflection alone cannot detect this historical mistake.
* All seven detected adjacent local pair records mirror correctly: the wardrobe lower/upper door pairs, the three balloon rear gate pairs, and the school bus lower/upper warped-door pairs. The gates do not have door hinges. Pair symmetry is not a claim about placement, opening direction, or intent; single/non-adjacent gates and doors are inventoried without assuming a partner.

## All 90 binary comparisons and conversion

The JSON independently parses each compressed Sponge v2 NBT, checks declared dimensions and DataVersion, decodes every x-fastest VarInt, and compares every palette-selected state to the source or history-converted source. It includes individual binary hashes, palette equality, voxel count, and every differing voxel. Of the 30 unsuffixed originals, 28 match current source at every voxel; the wardrobe original differs at four historical hinge positions and the bee original differs at the four corrected face/brow cells. All 30 1.21.4 and all 30 26.1 variants match their current source-derived states and palette sets: **no current variant was found stale**. This is a state-stream comparison, not byte identity, live placement or full NBT structural validation.

The 26.1 conversion uses the reviewed history converter rather than treating changed names as errors. Its six affected builds are bee 24, enderman 24, pig 24, greenhouse 19, steamboat 1, and grandfather clock 4 chain voxels. `chain` becomes `iron_chain`, retaining the axis and waterlogged state; the clock has two X-axis and two Y-axis cells. All conversion edges, source/target states and counts are in the JSON. The unchanged unsuffixed originals are not overwritten.

## Mechanical observations are separate

Symmetry does not determine whether a stair shape matches neighboring stairs, whether a carpet has support, or whether server updates will alter a state. As a separate deterministic voxel observation, the carousel has 80 carpet cells at y=3 with explicit air directly below at y=2 (for example (5,3,3)); these are support concerns, not evidence for any symmetry repair. A full stair-neighbor/placement-physics audit is outside this generator and no assertion is made that stair shapes were mechanically verified. Neither carpet nor stair architecture is automatically changed here; any mechanical findings require separate review, reference/placement evidence, and appropriate schematic regeneration.
